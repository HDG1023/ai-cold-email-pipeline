## Campaign: TiltIt Webinar Launch — Video Editing Agencies, USA

**Offer:** 3D animation software — edit 4K animations in <30 seconds  
**ICP:** Video editing agencies, 20–150 employees, USA  
**Result:** ~28% open rate, 9% reply rate, 3 demos booked from 14 contacts

---

## Sample Sequence — Contact: Marcus, Pixelcraft Studio

---

### Email 1 (Day 1)

**Subject A:** Marcus, your team is editing 3D the hard way  
**Subject B:** Pixelcraft + 4K 3D in 30 seconds?

```
Hi Marcus,

Noticed Pixelcraft is producing a lot of screen-recording content lately — great stuff.

The teams doing the most sophisticated 3D motion work right now are cutting production time by 80% with AI-assisted tools rather than manual compositing.

We built TiltIt specifically for agencies like yours — 4K 3D animations in under 30 seconds, no 3D experience needed.

Would a 15-min demo make sense this week?

Manthan
```

---

## Key Observations (for Claude to internalize)

- Every email opens with a specific, researched detail about the prospect
- The product is mentioned once — not in every email
- Tone is confident but never pushy
- CTA is always a simple yes/no or a request for a reply
- Subject lines are short and curiosity-driven
- Break-up email is warm, not passive-aggressive
